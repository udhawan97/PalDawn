# Community conduct

PalDawn welcomes careful collaboration across software, design, education,
and medicine. Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.

## Expected

- Critique the work, not the person.
- State uncertainty and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Protect privacy; never post patient or other sensitive personal data.
- Respect licensing, attribution, medical-review, and safety gates.
- Make space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, or attempts to bypass the project’s safety and provenance
controls are not welcome.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Report sensitive conduct
issues privately through GitHub rather than in a public issue.
