Recommended concept system

Threshold Companion

An open passage, a rising route, and one companion point. Shown at 16 px with simplified heavier strokes; recognition has not been user-tested.

Direction 02

Paired Passage

Two offset guardians make a corridor in negative space. Designed for small-size use; more architectural than warm.

Direction 03

Dawn Seam

A cut of negative space carries the route upward. More editorial, less companion-like.

Recommended concept system

Threshold Companion keeps the route open, places the companion point ahead, and avoids literal heart, sun, shield, or monogram forms.

  1. 01 · PassageAn open arch establishes a protected threshold without becoming a shield or literal body part.
  2. 02 · RouteA rising fork introduces movement through the passage while remaining abstract and non-anatomical.
  3. 03 · CompanionOne point sits ahead of the route as an orientation cue—not a sun, diagnosis, or approval mark.
