# PalDawn v0.6.1 — Study Recovery

This patch improves the public app's local study and recovery paths. It keeps
the existing Home, body, condition and study routes and does not add lessons,
medical claims, dependencies or storage schemas.

## Preserve local work

- Atlas edits, navigation and update preparation cannot overwrite another
  tab's durable study when the current tab holds a conflicting unsaved draft.
  The draft remains available to copy or export before reloading.
- A full 150-record Atlas study rejects an additional record without evicting
  existing notes or personal marks; editing and clearing records still work.
- The shared 2 MiB backup limit accommodates every bounded Atlas and First
  Light note, including multibyte text and escaped control characters, while
  rejecting larger imports before reading them. Schemas 1–3 remain supported.
- Scene failure, in-tab retry and scene-free continuation preserve memory-only First
  Light notes, checkpoints and saved-stage state instead of remounting an empty
  workspace. Recovery controls own input while the scene is unavailable.
  Before a full-page reload, choose **Continue without 3D** and copy or export
  unsaved work. A browser reload does not preserve memory-only data.

## Keep actions and feedback clear

- Study Desk and condition catalog dialogs own keyboard input and focus;
  background study shortcuts cannot operate through them.
- The body introduction heading has an unambiguous accessible name.
- A canceled or newer backup selection invalidates older asynchronous reads,
  preventing stale replacement previews from returning in ordinary import or
  preserved-data recovery.
- The Atlas skip link moves focus into the disease explorer while preserving
  the exact disease, step and optional structure address.
- Current Atlas storage, conflict and capacity feedback stays visible after
  sharing a step; earlier share feedback cannot mask an unsaved note.

## Harden export and delivery boundaries

- First Light Markdown exports keep private notes as literal text, consistent
  with Atlas export. A private heading, link, image or HTML fragment cannot
  become authored Markdown in the study document. Stored notes are unchanged.
- Optional service-worker cache match, open and write failures retain a usable
  network response instead of making an online fetch fail.
- Dependency-license evaluation preserves grouping and AND/OR requirements.
  A prohibited required license cannot be accepted because another partial
  branch is permissive; a separate complete permissive alternative remains
  valid. Unsupported or malformed syntax fails closed.

## Distribution and review limits

GitHub publishes this source-only release after CI and GitHub Pages pass for
the exact release commit. No native installer, signing or notarization is
provided; browser installation remains optional and browser-dependent.

The public body remains conceptual, synthetic and not to scale. Educational
explanations remain unreviewed. Anatomy Lab and the graphics workbenches stay
separate local candidates, excluded from the public Pages artifact. This patch
does not publish reference packs or grant qualified anatomy/clinical approval,
diagnosis, treatment guidance or medical certification. Automated Chromium
and WebKit checks do not establish native Safari, assistive-technology,
installed-PWA or physical-device acceptance.
