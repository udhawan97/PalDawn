# PalDawn v0.6.0 — Clear Routes

## A Home that explains where each route goes

- Home now leads with **Explore the body** and **Understand a condition**.
- The primary navigation exposes Home, Explore body, Conditions and My study.
- `#home`, `#body`, `#conditions` and `#study` can be reloaded and revisited with
  browser history.
- The body route states which model is public and which male/female reference
  experience remains a separately prepared local candidate.

## The 3D condition diagram is visible again

- Opening lung infection from Home no longer leaves the Home paper overlay over
  the condition scene.
- Whole-body mode fits the conceptual figure inside the study stage; selecting a
  highlighted structure still opens close focus and **Whole body** returns.
- Loading now has an announced status. A slow load offers a scene-free route,
  and the local reference landing has explicit retry and condition-study actions.

## Connected study routes

- **My study** exposes saved condition activity beside the existing First Light
  workspace without changing either storage schema.
- The local Anatomy Lab candidate can open the source-mapped lung reference from
  the lung pathway and return to the exact disease step.
- Anatomy backup replacement now restores its validated source selection in the
  current session.

## Boundaries

- The public 3D body remains conceptual, synthetic and not to scale.
- Disease explanations remain unreviewed educational synthesis.
- Anatomy Lab is still local-only and excluded from the normal Pages build.
  Male and female source assemblies are not a matched pair or complete anatomy.
- This release supplies no diagnosis, treatment guidance, medical approval or
  qualified anatomy/clinical sign-off.
