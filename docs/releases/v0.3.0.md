# PalDawn v0.3.0 — Mechanism Lens

Released 24 August 2026.

Mechanism Lens makes the current disease phase visible in the procedural body.
It adds a richer whole-body shell, layered organ surfaces, small system
scaffolds, phase-to-phase signal paths, and organ close focus while preserving
the complete Systems Atlas and First Light journeys.

## What is included

- Step-driven phase signals across the exact structures already authored for
  every disease mechanism step.
- Close focus for highlighted organs and systems, with a stable Whole body exit
  and automatic reset when the phase changes.
- Layered project-authored body, organ, vessel, nerve, skeleton, airway,
  digestive, urinary, optic, and cardiac visual detail.
- A visible Mechanism Lens readout connecting the explanation panel to the 3D
  anchor, selected focus, and structures represented in the phase.
- Lazy 3D loading plus a generated offline asset manifest that includes the
  split scene chunk.
- Responsive and reduced-motion behavior for the new visual system.

## Evidence and safety boundary

The visual model is illustrative, procedural, not to scale, and not reviewed
anatomy. The new phase signals encode current interface state; they are not a
pathology simulation, severity score, or treatment response. No third-party 3D
asset, new medical claim, patient data, diagnosis, risk calculation, or
treatment selection is included. The source-linked educational synthesis still
has not been reviewed by a named qualified clinician.
