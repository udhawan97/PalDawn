# PalDawn v0.2.0 — Systems Atlas

Released 22 August 2026.

Systems Atlas turns PalDawn into an English-only, evidence-first disease
learning environment while retaining the complete First Light engineering
voyage. Learners can start with ten conditions from the World Health
Organization's 2021 global causes-of-death ranking, inspect a conceptual 3D
systems map, and follow a detailed eleven-step diabetes mechanism.

## What is included

- Ten source-linked disease starting journeys with Plain English and Clinical
  terms reading depths.
- An eleven-step diabetes sequence from digestion and insulin signalling to
  acute metabolic effects, longer-term whole-body effects, and ongoing care.
- A project-authored procedural body map with orbit, organ selection, system
  highlights, and an exploded view.
- A responsive condition index, in-app guide, browser Back and Forward support,
  and an explicit journey-completion action.
- The original five-stage First Light voyage, local learner workspace, offline
  shell, accessibility preferences, and text-voyage fallback.

## Evidence and safety boundary

The disease text links directly to WHO and NIH/NIDDK sources checked on
22 August 2026. The synthesis has not been reviewed by a named qualified
clinician. The body is a conceptual systems map made from PalDawn-authored
procedural geometry; it is not reviewed anatomy and is not to scale. This
release does not diagnose symptoms, calculate personal risk, choose treatment,
or contain patient data or third-party anatomy assets.
